🤖 Appointment Automations
Automatically send reminders before appointments and follow-ups after — via WhatsApp, Email, Instagram or Messenger.
Enable Automations
When on, the system will automatically send reminder & follow-up messages at the configured times.
💡
How it works: When a customer books an appointment via any channel (WhatsApp, Email, Instagram, Messenger), the AI detects the intent and creates an appointment record automatically. Your configured reminder is then sent before the appointment and a follow-up is sent after — using your WhatsApp template.
