🤖 Appointment Automations
Automatically send reminders before appointments and follow-ups after — via WhatsApp using approved templates.
Enable Automations
When on, the scheduler will automatically send reminder & follow-up messages at configured times.
💡
How it works: When a customer books via any channel, the AI creates an appointment record. The scheduler Lambda (runs every 5 min) sends the reminder before and follow-up after using your WhatsApp templates.
🔔 Reminder Message
Sent before the appointment as a confirmation reminder.
E.g. 24 = send 1 day before the appointment.
Approved template name in Meta Business Manager.
📨 Follow-up Message
Sent after the appointment — thank the customer, collect feedback, or upsell.
E.g. 2 = send 2 hours after the appointment ends.
